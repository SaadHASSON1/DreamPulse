Privacy Policy — DreamPulse

Last updated: May 2026

DreamPulse ("we", "our", or "us") is developed by Saad Hasson (X13LABS). This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use DreamPulse on your Wear OS device.

1. Information We Collect

DreamPulse collects the following data solely to provide its core functionality:

  • Heart rate data — used to detect when you fall asleep and to schedule your alarm.
  • Motion/accelerometer data — used to detect stillness and movement during sleep monitoring.
  • Sleep session data — duration, start time, and wake time, stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Your Data

All data collected by DreamPulse is:

  • Processed and stored locally on your Wear OS device only.
  • Never transmitted to any external server or third party.
  • Never sold, shared, or used for advertising purposes.
  • Used exclusively to detect sleep onset and trigger your alarm at the right time.

3. Data Storage

DreamPulse does not use any cloud storage or remote databases. All sleep data, preferences, and session history remain on your device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all associated data.

4. Health Data

DreamPulse accesses heart rate and motion sensor data through the Android Health Services API. This data is used in real-time for sleep detection and is not stored beyond the current session summary shown to you after waking.

5. Permissions

DreamPulse requests the following permissions:

  • BODY_SENSORS — to read heart rate data for sleep detection.
  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — to monitor motion for stillness detection.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — to restore active alarms after device restart.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM — to fire your alarm at the precise scheduled time.
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE — to keep sleep monitoring active while your screen is off.
  • VIBRATE — to trigger alarm vibration on your wrist.
  • WAKE_LOCK — to ensure the alarm fires even when the device is in deep sleep.

6. Children's Privacy

DreamPulse is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
